THE PROCESS

Clear decisions before paid generation.

  1. 01

    Source review

    We review approved images, footage, listing facts, rights, brand details, and the intended audience.

  2. 02

    Direction

    You approve the story, format, aspect ratio, mood, shot order, and use of any conceptual imagery.

  3. 03

    Draft film

    We create a 480p review master, check accuracy and continuity, then revise the agreed direction.

  4. 04

    Finish and delivery

    After picture approval, we upscale, add deterministic graphics and sound, run QC, and deliver the agreed formats.
